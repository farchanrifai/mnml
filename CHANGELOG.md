# Changelog

What has changed in mnml, newest first. mnml started from Search 1.0.4 by
Office Commun; what came before is in Search's own history and in git.

## Unreleased

- The page floats as a rounded card on the window's frosted material, as in
  Dia and Arc; the chat beside it is a card too. The column, the strip and
  the chat slide in smoothly, full screen included, and ⌘E opens the chat
  at once.
- A tab in full screen is never put to sleep (it could crash).
- Quitting from a script or an installer no longer waits on "Quit mnml?".
