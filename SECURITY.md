# Security

mnml handles your passwords, your history and every page you open, so a
hole in it matters more than most bugs. If you find one, please report it
privately first: the repository's **Security** tab ›
[**Report a vulnerability**](https://github.com/farchanrifai/mnml/security/advisories/new).

Say what you found, where in the code, and how to see it happen. A proof of
concept that stays on your own machine is welcome; please don't try it on
other people's accounts or data.
